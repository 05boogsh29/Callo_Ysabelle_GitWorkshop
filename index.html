📖 ABOUT THE GAME

GAME BACKGROUND

Call of Duty: Mobile brings the legendary Call of Duty franchise experience to mobile gaming.

The game combines competitive multiplayer matches, battle royale survival, and customizable combat systems.

Players can experience different seasons, events, weapons, and challenges while building their own combat style.
